Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how League office / bAIseball (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use baiseball.app and related league office services.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address used to sign in
  • Optional mobile phone number, if you choose to save one for text messages
  • SMS opt-in consent timestamp when you agree to receive texts
  • Sign-in tokens, session tokens, and basic request logs needed to operate the service
  • League, team, lineup, rivalry, and schedule records you create

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, operate the league office, send one-time sign-in links by email or text when you request them, send related transactional account alerts you have opted into, and maintain the league administration data you enter.

3. Mobile phone numbers and text messages

If you provide a mobile number and opt in, we use that number only to send transactional League office texts, such as one-time sign-in links and account alerts. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is not sold. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties except our messaging providers as needed to deliver the texts you requested.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 8 messages per month, depending on how often you request a sign-in text or receive account alerts.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out: Reply STOP to cancel texts at any time, or remove your phone number on the Account page. Reply HELP for help, or email jeremyjbowers@gmail.com.

4. Service providers

We use processors that help us run the service:

  • DigitalOcean for hosting and the database
  • Mailgun for email delivery, when configured
  • Twilio for SMS delivery, when configured

These providers process information only to provide their services to us, under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Retention

Owner and league records remain until you delete them or ask us to remove them. Expired sign-in links and sessions are discarded after use or expiry. If you opt out of texts or remove your phone number, we stop using that number for messaging.

6. Your choices

  • Sign in by email without providing a phone number
  • Save or remove a phone number on the Account page
  • Reply STOP to opt out of texts
  • Email us to request access or deletion of your owner record

7. Children

League office is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
